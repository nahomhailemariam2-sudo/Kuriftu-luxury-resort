Our Comprehensive History & Legacy

Founded with a vision to transform East African tourism, Kuriftu Resort & Spa has spent over 15 years redefining luxury, environmental sustainability, and community-integrated hospitality. From our flagship resort on the shores of Lake Bishoftu, we have expanded into multi-faceted experiential hubs offering traditional African hydrotherapy, international culinary arts, and state-of-the-art family entertainment parks across Ethiopia.

Our Resort Branches Across Ethiopia

Kuriftu Resort & Spa Bishoftu (Debre Zeit)

The original lakeside sanctuary featuring private stone cottages, full-service African spa, boat excursions, and lush indigenous gardens.

Kuriftu Resort & Spa Lake Tana (Bahir Dar)

Perched on the magnificent source of the Blue Nile, offering sweeping views of Lake Tana, modern luxury suites, and historical island excursion packages.

Kuriftu Water Park Entoto (Addis Ababa)

East Africa's largest water amusement park located within the historic Entoto natural park, featuring massive wave pools, high-speed slides, and family food courts.

Kuriftu Resort Awash National Park

An immersive wilderness luxury experience situated right next to the Awash River waterfalls and wildlife savannahs.

Comprehensive Resort Rules, Policies & Photography Regulations

1. Check-In & Check-Out Policies

  • Standard hotel check-in time is strictly 2:00 PM local East African Time.
  • Check-out time must be completed by 12:00 PM noon to allow room sanitization.
  • Valid government-issued identification or passport is mandatory for all adult guests upon registration.

2. Water Park & Swimming Pool Safety Rules

  • Proper athletic swimwear (nylon or lycra) is strictly required. Loose clothing, denim, or street shoes are prohibited in pools.
  • Children under 12 years of age must be accompanied and actively supervised by an adult guardian at all times.
  • Diving, running on wet walkways, and rough play are strictly forbidden to prevent injuries.

3. Photography, Filming & Media Regulations

  • Commercial professional photoshoots and drone usage require prior written management approval and permit fees.
  • Casual personal photography and smartphone recording for private memories are warmly welcomed across all open resort gardens and pools.
  • Photography inside active spa treatment rooms, guest bedrooms, and restricted staff quarters is strictly prohibited to protect privacy.
